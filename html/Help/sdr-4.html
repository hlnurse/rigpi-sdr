SDR 4

 

 

SDR4 is the administrator's SDR dashboard. It displays up to four account SDR workspaces together, so you can view multiple configured receivers from one page.

Open the dashboard
 

1.Sign in with an administrator account.

2.Select SDR4 in the RigPi navigation bar.

3.Identify each workspace by the account callsign or username shown above it.

4.Use the controls inside a workspace to connect and operate its configured SDR.

Which accounts appear?

The dashboard uses the first four active accounts in user-ID order. Each workspace is opened with that account's SDR path and radio number. Empty positions display No SDR.
 

SDR4 presents the configured workspaces; it does not create additional hardware receivers. Simultaneous operation depends on the receivers and services available to those accounts.

Audio and a larger view

Start audio in the workspace you want to hear. If several workspaces play audio, their sound can overlap; stop audio in the others when listening to one receiver.
 

For a larger single-account workspace, open Elmer. See RigPi-SDR for tuning, spectrum, waterfall, audio and display controls.
