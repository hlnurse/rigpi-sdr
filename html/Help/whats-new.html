RigPi 5.0 Changes 10/1/2026

RigPi 5.0 highlights

•Ask Elmer answers questions about RigPi, operating frequencies, stations, clubs, callbook information, and other amateur-radio topics in 22 languages and regional variants. An attached image or pasted screenshot can be included with a question.

•Elmer Voice provides voice input and spoken answers in Ask Elmer and voice commands in Elmer Control.

•Elmer Control accepts informal text or voice commands to operate the radio from the Tuner and Keyer windows, including complete split-frequency operation.

•RigPi-SDR adds an integrated browser-based spectrum display, waterfall, receiver, memories, scanning, and remote-SDR access.

•WebRTC browser audio replaces Mumble with full-duplex audio, requiring no separate client.

•Native HTTPS with DuckDNS provides a free permanent HTTPS address when the station has a reachable public IP address and router port 443 can be forwarded. DuckDNS publishes the station's public IP address and generally cannot accept inbound connections through carrier-grade NAT (CGNAT).

•Cloudflare Tunnel support provides secure HTTPS remote access without router port forwarding and is the preferred alternative when CGNAT, blocked inbound ports, or public-IP concealment matters.

•Designed and optimized for the 64-bit Raspberry Pi 5 (strongly recommended). Raspberry Pi 4 can run RigPi 5 but provide substantially less processing headroom.

RigPi-SDR
 

•Integrated browser-based SDR panadapter with spectrum and waterfall displays

•Flexible spectrum and waterfall placement in the RigPi Tuner and CW Keyboard

•Adjustable spectrum floor, span, smoothing, and color palette

•LED-style frequency display with click-to-tune and scroll-wheel tuning

•Drag the spectrum to tune; drag passband edges to change bandwidth

•Bidirectional RigSync for transceiver frequency, mode, and bandwidth

•USB, LSB, USB-D, CW, CWR, AM, NFM, WFM, RTTY, and RTTYR demodulation, plus live FT8 and FT4 decoding

•SDR Support for Airspy HF+ Discovery, HackRF Pro (receive-only), RTL-SDR and Nooelec receivers, RX-888 MKII, and SDRplay receivers except the original RSP (through SDRconnect (running Headless)

•Automatic RX-888 MKII firmware loading at startup and USB reconnection, with SDR streaming resumed without restarting the server

•User-selectable or shared SDR devices

•Three-dimensional waterfall display with mouse, trackpad, and touchscreen navigation

•WebRTC two-way browser audio with VU meter, oscilloscope, and audio spectrum

•Audio with persistent left, center, and right stereo positioning

•ALSA Loopback audio for programs such as WSJT-X and Fldigi

•Twenty general frequency, mode, and bandwidth memories plus three-stack Rig Band memories

•Memory scanning, squelch-controlled activity recording, playback, and downloading

•Worldwide KiwiSDR and OpenWebRX receivers selected from a built-in map of more than 1,300 receivers

•Ask Elmer assistance with signal identification and operating suggestions

•Voice Keyer for contest operation

•Bug fixes for WinKeyer and CAT keying. Local sidetone provided for CAT and remote keying.
